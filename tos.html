1. What this service is

Clips GTA VI is a personal, non-commercial automation tool. It locates highlight moments in publicly available GTA VI gameplay videos, edits them into short vertical clips with burned-in captions, and — after a human manually reviews and approves each individual clip — publishes them to a single TikTok account operated by the developer.

This tool is operated by one individual for one TikTok account. It is not offered as a service to third parties, and no other TikTok account is connected to it without that account owner's explicit authorization through TikTok's own login flow.
