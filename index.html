BuffoonClips

A personal tool that turns long YouTube videos into short, vertical highlight clips — with every single clip watched and approved by a person before it's ever posted.

How it works

01

Find the highlightAudio and speech are analyzed to locate the most reactive, exciting moments in a long-form video.

02

Edit the clipThe moment is cropped to vertical video, framed around any visible speaker, and captioned automatically.

03

Human reviewEvery single clip is watched and manually approved or rejected by the developer before anything leaves as a draft.

Features

Automatic highlight detection

Combines audio energy and speech analysis to locate moments worth clipping.

Vertical reframing

Converts widescreen footage to 9:16 without cutting off the subject.

Auto captions

Burns in readable, word-by-word captions synced to the speech.

Draft-only delivery

Finished clips are sent to TikTok as a draft — nothing posts without a manual review.

About

BuffoonClips is operated by one individual for one TikTok account. It is not a public service — no other account connects to it, and no data beyond what's needed to prepare and deliver an approved clip is collected. See the Privacy Policy for details.

FAQ

Does this post automatically to TikTok?

No. Finished clips are delivered to the connected TikTok account as a draft. A person always reviews and taps Publish inside the TikTok app.

Whose videos are used?

Only publicly available YouTube videos. Short excerpts are used for highlight/commentary purposes, and the tool never claims ownership of the original footage.

Can other people use this?

Not currently — it's built and operated by one developer for one TikTok account.
