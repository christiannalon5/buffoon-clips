Privacy Policy

Effective 23 November 2026 — BuffoonClips

1. Overview

BuffoonClips is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by a single individual to prepare and deliver short edited clips, as drafts, to one TikTok account. It does not run for other users, does not serve ads, and does not sell or share data with third parties.

2. What TikTok data we access

Through TikTok's own login (OAuth), the tool requests only the permissions needed to identify the connected account and upload a video as a draft. It does not read, store, or process data belonging to that account's followers, viewers, or any other TikTok user.

DataPurpose
Upload-as-draft permission (OAuth token)Deliver approved clips to the connected account as a draft
Basic account identifierConfirm the token belongs to the intended account

3. What we store

Video files are processed and temporarily cached on a private, self-hosted server while a clip is being prepared and reviewed. The OAuth access token and refresh token for the single connected account are stored on that same private server and are never shared with third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

  • A clip rejected during manual review is deleted immediately.
  • A clip that is delivered as a draft is kept as a local backup for about 14 days after being marked as published, then deleted automatically — it continues to exist on TikTok itself.
  • Source video/audio downloaded to generate a clip is deleted once it is no longer needed to produce further clips from that video.

5. Your rights

Since the only TikTok account connected to this tool belongs to its own operator, data subject requests (access, correction, deletion) are handled directly by that same person. You can revoke this tool's access at any time from TikTok's own account settings (Settings → Security → Manage account → Apps and websites access), which immediately invalidates its stored tokens.

6. Children's privacy

This tool is not directed at children and is not available for signup by anyone other than its operator.

7. International data transfer

The server that processes and stores data for this tool is located in Spain (EU). No data is transferred outside of what TikTok's own API requires to operate the connected account.

8. What we don't do

No analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising, no data resale, and no access to data beyond what is listed above.

9. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.
