Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 — Clips GTA VI

1. Overview

Clips GTA VI is a personal, non-commercial tool operated by a single individual to publish short edited clips to one TikTok account. It does not run for other users, does not serve ads, and does not sell or share data with third parties.

2. What TikTok data we access

Through TikTok's own login (OAuth), the tool requests only the permission needed to publish videos to the single connected account. It does not read, store, or process data belonging to that account's followers, viewers, or any other TikTok user.

DataPurpose
Content posting permission (OAuth token)Upload approved clips to the connected account
Basic account identifierConfirm the token belongs to the intended account

3. What we store

Video files are processed and temporarily cached on a private, self-hosted server while a clip is being prepared and reviewed. The OAuth token for the single connected account is stored on that same private server and is never shared with third parties.

4. Retention and deletion

  • A clip rejected during manual review is deleted immediately.
  • A clip that is published is kept as a local backup for about 14 days, then deleted automatically — it continues to exist on TikTok itself.

5. What we don't do

No analytics or tracking scripts, no advertising, no data resale, and no access to data beyond what is listed above.

6. Changes

This policy may be updated from time to time. The effective date above reflects the latest revision.
